A practical partner for your organization.

Shubhkiran Quality Forum is a consultancy and training organization of experienced auditors and engineers. The work spans management systems, audit readiness, professional development, and technical and sustainability requirements.

Led by Mr. Vilas Shinde, a mechanical engineer and lead auditor, SQF focuses on customized solutions, hands-on implementation, and knowledge transfer. An extensive network helps the practice assist organizations across India and support needs wherever they operate.
