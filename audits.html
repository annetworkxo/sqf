Second-party audits

Agreed criteria. Actionable findings.

Assess sites against a checklist that reflects your requirements. Use the findings to identify gaps, review performance, and plan follow-up.

  • Suppliers, manufacturers and warehouses
  • Checklists updated when requirements change
  • Annual evaluation of each site’s performance

How it works

A practical audit cycle.

  1. 01

    Agree the checklist

    Define the quality, safety and environmental criteria to be assessed.

  2. 02

    Assess the site

    Evaluate the site against the agreed scope and identify gaps.

  3. 03

    Review performance

    Use results to guide periodic assessments and annual site evaluation.

  4. 04

    Update and follow up

    Amend criteria when requirements change and track improvement needs.
